About
Kyle Knee
Mathematics Department, Harper College

Kyle Knee is a mathematics professor in the Mathematics Department at Harper College in Palatine, IL, where he has taught for over a decade. He holds a Master's in the Teaching of Mathematics from the University of Illinois.

He teaches primarily across the Calculus sequence, along with Statistics, Precalculus, Linear Algebra, and Discrete Mathematics.

He built this site to bring his interactive applets, lecture materials, and practice worksheets together in one place — for his own students and anyone else who finds them useful.
